## Real Canadian Pricing Breakdown for Home Golf Simulators

Understanding realistic costs in Canadian Dollars (CAD) eliminates unexpected expenses associated with currency conversion, cross-border shipping, and local installation labor.

### Canadian Pricing Tiers

1. **Entry-Level Budget ($2,500 – $5,000 CAD)**: Rapsodo MLM2PRO or Garmin Approach R10 paired with a net/DIY enclosure, iPad or basic laptop display, and entry hitting mat.
2. **Mid-Range Sweet Spot ($7,500 – $14,000 CAD)**: SkyTrak+ or Foresight GC3, dedicated Carl's Place impact enclosure, 1080p short-throw laser projector, high-spec gaming PC, and GSPro software.
3. **Tour & Commercial Grade ($20,000 – $45,000+ CAD)**: Foresight GCQuad / GCQuadMAX or Uneekor EYE XO2 overhead mount, custom padded enclosure with plush turf flooring, 4K laser projector, and full professional installation.
