## Building a Basement Golf Simulator in Canada

Canadian basements offer ideal climate control and year-round playability, making them premier candidate spaces for home simulator installs.

### Key Considerations for Basement Builds

- **HVAC Duct & Bulkhead Obstructions**: Measure ceiling height to the lowest hanging duct or beam, not just the floor joists. Rerouting flexible ducting often recovers 6+ inches of vital swing height.
- **Concrete Subfloors**: Direct hitting on concrete damages joints and wrists over time. Always layer shock-absorbing EVA foam tiles or Dricore subfloor panels beneath your turf.
- **Acoustics & Sound Dampening**: Ball impacts on screens create sudden acoustic spikes. Foam wall padding dampens sound transmission to upper living areas.
