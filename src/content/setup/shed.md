## Building a Dedicated Outdoor Golf Simulator Shed in Canada

When indoor room dimensions or basement ceiling heights fall short, constructing a purpose-built timber shed or outbuilding provides a dedicated sanctuary for year-round golf.

### Structural Requirements

Building an outbuilding in Canadian municipalities requires proper permit compliance, foundation frost depth planning, and robust insulation. Vaulted ceiling trusses avoid low-hanging crossbeams, yielding maximum swing confidence for tall players.
