## Building a DIY Golf Simulator in Canada

Building your own home golf simulator is one of the most rewarding home improvement projects for Canadian golfers. By assembling an EMT conduit frame and selecting standalone launch monitors like the SkyTrak+ or Foresight GC3, you can achieve a commercial-quality simulator experience at a fraction of full turnkey costs.

### Total DIY Budget Breakdown (CAD)

- **EMT Conduit Frame & Fittings**: $250 – $400 CAD
- **Impact Screen**: $350 – $800 CAD
- **Side Safety Netting & Padding**: $150 – $300 CAD
- **Quality Hitting Mat**: $400 – $800 CAD
- **Entry / Mid Launch Monitor**: $2,000 – $4,500 CAD
- **Short-Throw Projector**: $800 – $1,800 CAD

### DIY Tips for Canadian Sourcing

Sourcing materials locally saves substantial shipping costs. EMT conduit pipes are widely stocked in standard 10-foot lengths at Canadian hardware retailers. Ensure your impact screen uses heavy-duty polyester woven fabric designed specifically for high-velocity ball impacts.
