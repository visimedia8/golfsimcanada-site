## Minimum & Recommended Room Dimensions for Canadian Builds

Room dimensions determine hardware selection and swing safety. Measure your room accurately in feet and inches before purchasing enclosures or launch monitors.

| Dimension | Absolute Minimum | Recommended | Commercial Ideal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ceiling Height** | 8.5 ft (2.6m) | 9.5 ft (2.9m) | 10.5 ft (3.2m) |
| **Room Width** | 10.0 ft (3.0m) | 12.5 ft (3.8m) | 15.0 ft (4.6m) |
| **Room Depth** | 12.0 ft (3.6m) | 16.0 ft (4.9m) | 18.0 ft (5.5m) |

### Photometric vs. Radar Space Impact

- **Photometric Camera Systems (Foresight GC3 / GCQuad, SkyTrak+)**: Sit alongside the ball. Require minimal room depth (only ball-to-screen distance needed, ~8–10 ft).
- **Radar Systems (TrackMan 4, Full Swing KIT)**: Sit behind the player. Require 8 ft behind the ball plus 8–10 ft from ball to screen (16–18 ft total depth).
