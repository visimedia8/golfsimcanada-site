## How to Select the Right Launch Monitor in Canada

The launch monitor is the technological brain of your golf simulator. Selecting the right model depends heavily on room geometry, lighting conditions, and software preferences.

### Key Launch Monitor Categories

- **Floor Photometric (Foresight GC3, SkyTrak+, Bushnell)**: High precision indoor tracking, zero room depth wasted.
- **Overhead Photometric (Uneekor EYE XO / XO2, Foresight Falcon)**: Mounted out of harm's way on ceilings. Seamless for left-handed and right-handed players.
- **Radar (TrackMan 4, Mevo+, Full Swing KIT)**: Superior outdoors and in large room footprints.
