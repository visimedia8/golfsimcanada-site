## Designing a Canadian Garage Golf Simulator

The garage is the most popular installation location for Canadian home simulators. However, sub-zero winter temperatures across provinces like Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, Ontario, and Quebec create unique engineering requirements for garage golf builds.

### Crucial Garage Simulator Factors

1. **Ceiling & Garage Door Tracks**: Standard garage door tracks hang low. Upgrading to a high-lift track or side-mount opener reclaims 12 to 24 inches of vertical clearance.
2. **Winter Climate & Heating**: Launch monitor electronics and optical sensors require operating temperatures above 5°C (41°F). Infrared ceiling-mounted heaters keep both golfers and equipment comfortable.
3. **Multi-Use Parking**: If you need to park cars in the garage during snowstorms, utilize retractable side curtains and a ceiling roll-up impact screen.
