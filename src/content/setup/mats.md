## Choosing Joint-Friendly Hitting Mats in Canada

Hitting thousands of golf balls off cheap artificial turf placed on hard concrete can cause lead wrist tendonitis, elbow pain, and joint fatigue.

### Recommended Hitting Surface Design

Choose mats with dedicated shock-absorbing hitting strips (such as Fiberbuilt grass density or Holy Grail hitting inserts) that allow the club head to pass through impact naturally without harsh vibrations.
