## Simulator Software Suites Compared for Canadian Players

Software renders course graphics, ball flight physics, and multi-player online competition.

### Top Software Platforms

- **GSPro ($250 USD / ~$340 CAD annually)**: Community favorite with 700+ user-created courses (including famous Canadian courses like Cabot Cliffs, Banff Springs, and St. George's), realistic physics, and active online tour leagues.
- **Foresight FSX Play & FSX 2020**: Premium photorealistic graphics optimized natively for Foresight GC series hardware.
- **E6 Connect**: Excellent cross-platform engine with broad launch monitor compatibility.
