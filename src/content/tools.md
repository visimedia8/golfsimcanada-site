Welcome to our suite of interactive tools designed specifically for Canadian indoor golfers. Whether you're planning a DIY basement build or looking for the perfect launch monitor, our calculators will help you make data-driven decisions.

## Available Tools

1. [**Simulator Room Builder**](/build/)  
   Calculate clearance requirements, screen sizes, and projector throw distance for your garage or basement.
2. [**Budget Calculator**](/tools/budget-calculator/)  
   Input your exact budget in CAD to get custom recommendations for launch monitors, enclosures, and accessories.
3. [**Product Comparator**](/tools/product-comparator/)  
   Compare two launch monitors head-to-head to see which is best for your specific use case.
4. [**Software Matchmaker**](/tools/software-matchmaker/)  
   Find the perfect simulation software (GSPro, E6, Awesome Golf, etc.) based on how you play.
5. [**Installer Quote Estimator**](/tools/quote-estimator/)  
   Get instant ballpark estimates for turnkey professional installations in your Canadian province.
