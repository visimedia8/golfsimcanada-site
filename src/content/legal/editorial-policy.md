# Editorial Policy

**Effective Date:** July 29, 2026

At GolfSimCanada, our mission is to provide accurate, unbiased, and comprehensive information to help Canadians build their perfect indoor golf setups. 

## How We Test Simulators

We take our testing methodology seriously. When we review launch monitors, enclosures, impact screens, and software, we evaluate them on several critical factors:
- **Accuracy and Reliability:** We verify data points like spin rate, ball speed, and launch angle.
- **Ease of Setup:** We assess how realistic it is for the average DIYer to install the product.
- **Software Integration:** We look at compatibility with major software platforms like GSPro, TGC 2019, and E6 Connect.
- **Value for Canadian Buyers:** We factor in exchange rates, shipping to Canada, duty fees, and local support options.

## Editorial Independence

While we participate in affiliate programs (as detailed in our [Disclaimer](/legal/disclaimer)), our editorial content is strictly independent. 
- **No Paid Placements:** We do not accept money from brands in exchange for positive reviews or favorable rankings.
- **Honest Feedback:** If a product has flaws, poor customer service, or inaccurate data, we will highlight those issues in our content. 
- **Objective Rankings:** Our "Best Of" lists are determined solely by our testing, research, and expertise, not by the commission rate of the product.

We are committed to maintaining your trust by delivering honest, expert-driven reviews.
