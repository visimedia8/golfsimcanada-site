## Indoor Golf Across Canada: Public Lounges to Home Builds

Indoor golf provides Canadian golfers with uninterrupted practice during the 5 to 6 months of winter when outdoor courses are closed.

Whether in Toronto, Vancouver, Calgary, Edmonton, Ottawa, Winnipeg, or Montreal, indoor golf lounges offer hourly bay rentals, winter leagues, and licensed food & beverage amenities.
