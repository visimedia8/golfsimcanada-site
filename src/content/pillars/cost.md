## Complete Canadian Golf Simulator Budget Breakdown

Building an indoor golf simulator in Canada involves several hardware and software components. Here is a itemized cost breakdown in Canadian Dollars (CAD).

### Itemized Equipment Cost Matrix (CAD)

| Component | Entry Level | Mid-Range Sweet Spot | Commercial Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Launch Monitor** | $800 – $2,500 CAD | $3,999 – $9,500 CAD | $15,000 – $28,000 CAD |
| **Enclosure & Screen** | $400 – $800 CAD | $1,200 – $2,500 CAD | $3,500 – $7,000 CAD |
| **Projector** | $600 – $1,200 CAD | $1,200 – $2,200 CAD | $3,000 – $6,000 CAD |
| **Hitting Mat & Turf** | $300 – $500 CAD | $600 – $1,200 CAD | $1,500 – $3,000 CAD |
| **Gaming PC / iPad** | iPad or $1,000 PC | $1,500 – $2,200 PC | $2,500 – $4,000 PC |
| **Software Subscriptions** | $0 – $300 CAD/yr | $340 CAD/yr (GSPro) | $1,000+ CAD/yr |

### Hidden Costs to Watch in Canada

- **US Dollar Exchange & Bank Fees**: Buying from US sites incurs a 2.5% to 3.5% credit card conversion markup.
- **Customs & Brokerage Duties**: Courier brokerage fees on international freight can add $200–$500 CAD unexpectedly.
- **Electrical & Heating**: Wiring a dedicated 20A circuit or installing a 240V garage heater typically costs $500–$1,500 CAD in electrician fees.
