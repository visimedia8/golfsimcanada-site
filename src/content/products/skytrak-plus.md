# SkyTrak+ (Plus) Launch Monitor Review: A Massive Upgrade

The original SkyTrak revolutionized the home golf simulator market. For years, it was the only affordable camera-based launch monitor that could fit in a small space. However, as technology advanced and competitors entered the market, the original SkyTrak began to show its age—especially regarding shot delay, misreads, and lack of club data.

Enter the **SkyTrak+ (Plus)**. Released to address every shortcoming of its predecessor, the SkyTrak+ combines photometric (camera) technology with dual-doppler radar to deliver a powerhouse unit at a mid-range price. For Canadian golfers, this might be the most compelling option under $5,000.

## 1. Dual-Technology: Camera Meets Radar

The biggest selling point of the SkyTrak+ is its hybrid data collection system.
- **The Camera System:** Like the original, high-speed cameras capture the ball at impact to measure ball speed, launch angle, backspin, and side spin. This ensures the unit remains highly accurate indoors in small spaces.
- **The Dual-Doppler Radar:** This is the game-changer. The radar tracks the clubface before and through impact, providing legitimate club data without needing reflective stickers.

### What Data Do You Get?
You now get a comprehensive suite of data previously reserved for units twice the price:
- Ball Speed, Launch Angle, Spin Rate, Spin Axis
- Carry and Total Distance
- **New:** Clubhead Speed, Smash Factor, Club Path, Face Angle, and Face-to-Path.

## 2. SkyTrak+ Pricing and Subscriptions in Canada

When evaluating the SkyTrak+ in Canada, you have to account for both the hardware and the ongoing software subscriptions.

### Hardware Cost
- The SkyTrak+ retails for roughly $2,995 USD, which translates to about **$4,000 - $4,200 CAD** depending on the exchange rate and import duties.

### Software Plans
SkyTrak operates on a tiered subscription model:
1. **Basic Plan (Free):** Simple driving range. No simulator integration.
2. **Game Improvement Plan (~$130 CAD/year):** Unlocks advanced range features, bag mapping, skills assessment, and is *required* to connect to third-party software.
3. **Play and Improve Plan (~$260 CAD/year):** Includes WGT (World Golf Tour) and 15 E6 Connect courses.

To play on software like GSPro, TGC 2019, or E6, you *must* have at least the Game Improvement plan. 

## 3. Simulator Space Requirements

Because it relies primarily on its camera for ball flight, the SkyTrak+ is a dream for tight Canadian basements. You do not need the 16-20 feet of depth required by pure radar units like the Garmin R10 or Mevo+. 

Place the unit a few inches from the ball, ensure you have enough room to swing, and you are good to go. Like the GC3 or Quad, the only negative is that right- and left-handed players must move the unit back and forth during a round.

## 4. Performance Enhancements Over the Original

If you are a Canadian golfer wondering if you should upgrade from the original SkyTrak, the answer is a resounding yes.
- **No More Shot Delay:** The infamous 2-3 second delay of the original is gone. The SkyTrak+ displays shots almost instantly.
- **Fewer Misreads:** The new camera system and processor mean a significantly lower percentage of dropped shots or unread chips.
- **Outdoor Viability:** The original SkyTrak was notoriously bad outdoors on grass or mats due to sunlight interference. The SkyTrak+ has improved outdoor performance, making it usable at the driving range during the summer.

## 5. Software Integrations

The SkyTrak+ connects natively to several top-tier simulator platforms:
- **E6 Connect:** Highly polished, great graphics, easy to use.
- **TGC 2019 (The Golf Club):** Massive library of over 100,000 user-created courses.
- **GSPro:** The current king of simulator software. While originally a workaround, SkyTrak and GSPro have integrated well, making this the preferred setup for most serious sim golfers.

## Conclusion

The SkyTrak+ successfully bridges the gap between entry-level monitors (like the Garmin R10) and high-end units (like the GC3). At roughly $4,200 CAD, it offers club data, near-instant shot rendering, and incredible indoor accuracy. For the average Canadian golfer looking to build a high-quality, reliable home simulator in a confined space, the SkyTrak+ is currently one of the best value propositions on the market.
