# Full Swing KIT: The Tiger-Approved Radar for Canadian Golfers

When the greatest golfer of his generation demands a launch monitor built to his exacting standards, the industry pays attention. The **Full Swing KIT** entered the market with the heaviest of endorsements: it was co-developed with and is heavily utilized by Tiger Woods. 

Designed to challenge the dominance of established commercial radar units, the Full Swing KIT aims to deliver tour-level accuracy in a sleek, highly portable, and user-friendly package. For Canadian golfers assessing the mid-to-high-tier launch monitor market, the KIT presents a fascinating alternative. In this review, we examine its radar technology, its indoor/outdoor capabilities, and how it fits into a Canadian home simulator build.

## 1. Machine-Learning Enhanced Radar

The Full Swing KIT is a Doppler radar-based launch monitor. It sits directly behind the golfer on the target line, tracking the clubhead and the ball through the impact zone and down the range.

### The Power of Micro-Doppler
What sets the KIT apart is its proprietary Micro-Doppler radar technology, combined with advanced machine learning algorithms. Full Swing spent years feeding the system millions of swings, allowing the AI to refine its calculations and predict ball flight with astonishing accuracy. This results in incredibly precise data capture, particularly in outdoor environments.

### The 4K Internal Camera
The KIT is not solely reliant on radar. It features a built-in 4K camera. While primarily used for high-resolution swing recording (allowing you to review your mechanics alongside your data), the camera also assists the radar in precisely locating the ball and aligning the unit to the target. 

## 2. Tour-Level Data Metrics

Tiger Woods requires absolute precision, and the KIT delivers 16 points of highly accurate data instantly. 

**Core Metrics:**
- Carry Distance, Total Distance
- Ball Speed, Club Speed, Smash Factor
- Spin Rate, Spin Axis
- Launch Angle, Launch Direction
- Apex Height
- Club Path, Face Angle, Face to Path
- Attack Angle, Dynamic Loft

The data accuracy outdoors is universally praised, standing toe-to-toe with units costing significantly more. Indoors, as with all radar systems, there are caveats.

## 3. Indoor Performance and Space Requirements

Bringing the Full Swing KIT indoors for the Canadian winter requires careful spatial planning. 

### The Radar Space Tax
Because the KIT sits behind the golfer, it requires significant room depth to operate effectively. 
- **Placement:** The unit must be positioned 10 feet behind the golf ball.
- **Flight Distance:** It requires a minimum of 8 feet of ball flight from the ball to the screen to calculate accurate spin. 
- **Total Depth:** A functional indoor KIT setup requires a room depth of at least **18 to 20 feet**. 

For many Canadian basements, 10 feet of space *behind* the tee is a dealbreaker. The KIT is often much better suited to deep garage simulator builds. 

### Indoor Spin Accuracy and Titleist RCT
Early iterations of the KIT software struggled with indoor spin calculations. However, Full Swing aggressively updated the firmware and, crucially, integrated support for **Titleist RCT (Radar Capture Technology) golf balls**. 

Using RCT balls indoors with the KIT completely changes the dynamic. The embedded radar reflectors allow the KIT to measure spin rates with immense accuracy, eliminating the erratic indoor readings that plague many radar systems. If you use a KIT indoors, RCT balls are essential.

## 4. The OLED Display: A Massive Outdoor Advantage

One of the standout hardware features of the Full Swing KIT is its vibrant, full-color OLED display built directly into the front of the unit. 

### Instant Visual Feedback
When you take the KIT to the driving range, you do not need to constantly look at your phone or tablet. The customizable OLED screen instantly displays your chosen data metrics in large, bright fonts after every swing. This allows for an uninterrupted, focused practice session. It is arguably the best on-device display in the launch monitor market.

## 5. Software and Simulation Ecosystem

The Full Swing KIT utilizes a dedicated app and integrates with top-tier simulation software.

### The Full Swing App
The companion iOS app (currently Apple only, a limitation for Android users) is beautifully designed. It stores your historical data, allows you to review your 4K swing videos, and presents shot dispersion charts clearly. The app experience is polished and user-friendly.

### E6 Connect Integration
For indoor simulation, the Full Swing KIT integrates natively with **E6 Connect**. E6 is renowned for its high-quality graphics, realistic physics, and massive library of world-class courses. The KIT connects via Wi-Fi to your PC or iOS device running E6, providing a seamless and highly enjoyable simulator experience. 

*Note: While E6 Connect is fantastic, the KIT does not currently officially support GSPro, which is a major consideration for many Canadian simulator builders who prefer GSPro's community and pricing model.*

## 6. Left and Right-Handed Play

Because the KIT is positioned 10 feet behind the tee on the target line, it is perfectly suited for multiplayer simulator environments. Left-handed and right-handed players can seamlessly take turns hitting without anyone needing to move or realign the launch monitor. This makes it an excellent choice for families or groups of friends with mixed handedness.

## 7. The Canadian Verdict: Is the KIT Right for You?

The Full Swing KIT sits in a highly competitive price bracket, positioned above entry-level radars but below the ultra-premium commercial units. 

**Pros:**
- Incredible outdoor accuracy, validated by the best players in the world.
- Stunning built-in OLED display for phone-free outdoor practice. 
- 4K swing video recording built-in.
- Frictionless left/right-handed indoor play.
- Excellent E6 Connect integration.

**Cons:**
- Massive indoor space requirements (10 feet behind the ball).
- Requires RCT balls for optimal indoor spin accuracy.
- iOS only companion app (No Android support). 
- No official GSPro integration.

**Conclusion:**
For the Canadian golfer, the Full Swing KIT is a niche but powerful option. If you are building a simulator in a tight basement, the space requirements will immediately rule it out. 

However, if you have a deep garage, value effortless left/right-handed play, and want a tour-grade practice tool to take to the range in the summer with a built-in display, the Full Swing KIT is a phenomenal piece of engineering. It delivers the premium data and build quality demanded by Tiger Woods, making it a standout choice for the serious, space-rich golfer.
