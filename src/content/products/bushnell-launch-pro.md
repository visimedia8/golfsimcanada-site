# Bushnell Launch Pro: The Canadian Conundrum

The Bushnell Launch Pro (BLP) is one of the most talked-about golf launch monitors in the world. Built on the exact same hardware platform as the highly acclaimed Foresight GC3, the BLP offers a different pricing model designed to lower the barrier to entry.

However, for Canadian golfers, the Bushnell Launch Pro has been a source of immense frustration due to geo-restrictions, complex subscription models, and limited availability. In this guide, we break down what the BLP is, whether you can get it in Canada, and if it's worth the hassle.

## 1. What is the Bushnell Launch Pro?

The BLP is a photometric (camera-based) launch monitor. It uses a three-camera system to capture ball and club data at impact. In fact, if you take the casing off a BLP and a Foresight GC3, you will find identical hardware. Foresight Sports and Bushnell Golf are owned by the same parent company, Vista Outdoor.

### The Pricing Model Difference
- **Foresight GC3:** High upfront cost (approx. $7,500+ CAD) with all software and data unlocked permanently. No subscriptions required.
- **Bushnell Launch Pro:** Lower upfront cost (approx. $2,000 - $3,500 USD depending on the model) but requires a hefty annual subscription to access simulator features and advanced data.

## 2. The Canadian Problem: Geo-Blocking and Subscriptions

Here is the most critical information for Canadian consumers: **The Bushnell Launch Pro is officially intended for the US market only.**

### Purchasing Challenges
You generally cannot buy a BLP directly from Bushnell or authorized US retailers and have it shipped to Canada. Canadians who own a BLP typically have to:
1. Ship it to a US P.O. Box or a cross-border shipping service.
2. Drive across the border to pick it up and pay duties on the way back.

### The Subscription Nightmare
The bigger issue is software. To use the BLP as anything more than a basic screen-less monitor, you need a subscription (Gold subscription is currently $499 USD/year). 
- In the past, Bushnell geo-blocked Canadian IP addresses, preventing Canadians from activating the software or renewing subscriptions. 
- While some users bypass this using VPNs, it is a significant risk. Imagine spending $4,000 CAD on a device only to have Bushnell lock your account because they detected a Canadian credit card or IP address.

## 3. Performance and Specs

If you do manage to get a BLP working in Canada, the performance is spectacular.

**Ball Data:**
- Ball Speed
- Launch Angle
- Total Spin, Backspin, Side Spin
- Spin Axis

**Club Data (Requires Club Data model or Gold Subscription):**
- Clubhead Speed
- Smash Factor
- Club Path
- Angle of Attack

Because it is camera-based, it requires very little space—perfect for standard Canadian basement setups. It needs no flight path to calculate spin; it physically photographs the dimples on the golf ball.

## 4. Software: FSX Play and GSPro

With the Gold Subscription, you get access to Foresight’s FSX Play and FSX 2020. 
Furthermore, the Gold Subscription officially unlocks third-party API access, meaning you can plug the BLP into **GSPro**, the premier simulator software on the market. For many, a BLP running GSPro is the ultimate, most realistic indoor golf experience available.

## 5. Alternatives for Canadians

Given the headaches of importing and maintaining a BLP in Canada, what are the alternatives?

1. **Foresight GC3:** This is the most logical choice. It is exactly the same machine, fully supported in Canada by distributors like Par2Pro, and has zero subscription fees. Yes, the upfront cost is higher, but after 4-5 years of paying the BLP's $500/year subscription, the costs equalize.
2. **SkyTrak+:** Priced similarly to the BLP’s upfront cost, the SkyTrak+ is fully supported in Canada, offers club and ball data, and integrates with GSPro (with a much cheaper ~$130 CAD subscription).
3. **Uneekor EYE MINI Lite:** Another fantastic camera-based unit in the mid-tier price range that is officially available to Canadians.

## Conclusion

The Bushnell Launch Pro is an incredible piece of technology at a disruptive price point. However, the geo-restrictions and strict US-only subscription model make it a highly risky proposition for Canadian golfers. Unless you have a dual-residence or are comfortable managing VPNs and US billing addresses, we highly recommend purchasing the Foresight GC3 or a SkyTrak+ to avoid the border headaches.
