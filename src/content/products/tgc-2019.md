# TGC 2019 Golf Simulator Software: The Infinite Course Library for Canadian Golfers

When Canadian golfers look for simulator software to survive the brutal off-season, one title consistently dominates the conversation: **The Golf Club 2019 (TGC 2019)**. Known for its staggering library of over 150,000 user-generated courses, TGC 2019 offers unparalleled variety. 

This deep-dive review, structured for maximum topical authority, covers everything you need to know about running TGC 2019 in Canada, from hardware compatibility and local pricing to graphics, physics, and its legendary course designer.

## What is TGC 2019?

Developed by HB Studios (a Canadian company based in Nova Scotia) and integrated for simulation by ProTee United, TGC 2019 started as a console/PC video game and was adapted into a full-fledged simulator platform. Its defining feature is the underlying course creation engine, which has allowed a dedicated community to meticulously recreate almost every famous golf course in the world, including hundreds of local Canadian tracks.

### Key Value Propositions
- **Unlimited Courses:** With over 150,000 courses, you will never play the same layout twice unless you want to.
- **One-Time Purchase:** Unlike competitors pushing hefty annual subscriptions, TGC 2019 offers a highly attractive one-time lifetime license.
- **Massive Community:** Active online tours (like the simulator version of TGCTours) and forums.
- **Lidar Course Design:** Advanced users import topographical Lidar data to create 1:1 replicas of real-world courses.

## Canadian Pricing and Licensing

One of the biggest advantages of TGC 2019 is its pricing model. In a market moving rapidly toward subscriptions, TGC 2019 stands out.

### The Lifetime License
Purchasing TGC 2019 is generally done via a one-time payment. Through Canadian retailers or direct from ProTee, the cost is typically **$1,200 to $1,400 CAD** (depending on the exchange rate from the standard $895 USD price). 

- **No Annual Fees:** Once you buy it, you own it. All 150,000+ courses are available immediately without further paywalls.
- **Resale Value:** Licenses can sometimes be transferred (subject to ProTee's terms and transfer fees), retaining value if you upgrade later.

### Subscription Option
Some vendors offer an annual subscription for around **$650 CAD/year**. For almost all Canadian users planning to use their simulator for more than two winters, the lifetime license is mathematically the only logical choice.

## Hardware Compatibility

TGC 2019 is highly adaptable, supporting a wide array of launch monitors popular in the Canadian market.

### Supported Monitors
1. **SkyTrak:** Historically the most popular pairing for TGC 2019 in home garages.
2. **FlightScope Mevo+:** Excellent integration, making it a powerful mid-tier setup.
3. **Uneekor (EYE XO, QED, EYE MINI):** Flawless integration, providing premium tracking with unlimited courses.
4. **ProTee Systems:** Native support, naturally.
5. **Garmin Approach R10:** Supported via third-party workarounds and API integrations (though users should verify current status before purchasing solely for R10).
6. **Foresight GC2/GC3/Quad:** Supported, often via third-party integration tools.

## Graphics and Visual Engine

Because TGC 2019 was released in 2018/2019, its graphics engine is older than modern heavyweights like GSPro or E6 Connect.

- **Resolution:** Supports up to 4K resolution, which still looks excellent on large impact screens.
- **Lighting and Textures:** While trees and water lack the hyper-realism of Unreal Engine 5, the overall aesthetic is clean, vibrant, and perfectly acceptable for simulation.
- **Performance:** Because the engine is older, it is highly optimized. You don't need a cutting-edge PC to run it smoothly.

### System Requirements
- **OS:** Windows 10 64Bit
- **Processor:** Intel Core i5 @ 3.20GHz or AMD FX-8150
- **Memory:** 8 GB RAM
- **Graphics:** NVidia GTX 1060 (or better)
- **Hard Drive:** 30 GB available space

*Canadian Context:* A modest gaming PC from Best Buy or Canada Computers for roughly $1,000 CAD will easily max out TGC 2019 settings at 1080p, saving you thousands on hardware compared to newer software.

## Gameplay and Physics

### Ball Physics
TGC 2019 relies on the data sent by your launch monitor. The in-game physics translate this data accurately for the most part. Draw, fade, backspin, and topspin are represented well.
- **Critique:** Some users find the first bounce and roll out on firm fairways to be slightly exaggerated compared to real life.

### Putting Mechanics
Putting on TGC 2019 is notoriously challenging to master. The greens can be lightning fast, and reading the grid requires practice. Many users in simulator leagues opt to use automatic putting (gimmes based on proximity to the hole) to speed up play.

### Forgiving Elements
TGC 2019 allows you to adjust course conditions (firmness, speed, wind) and provides options to boost ball speed, which is great for kids or beginners using the simulator.

## The Course Library: Lidar and Canadian Content

The true magic of TGC 2019 is the community-driven course library. 

### Lidar Recreations
Designers use open-source Lidar data to map real-world elevation changes down to the centimeter. This means the slopes, bunkers, and greens are exact replicas of the real locations.

### Canadian Courses Available
If you want to play Canadian golf in the middle of February, TGC 2019 is your best bet. A quick search in the database reveals incredible recreations of:
- **Cabot Cliffs & Cabot Links** (Nova Scotia)
- **Banff Springs** (Alberta)
- **St. George's Golf and Country Club** (Ontario)
- **Glen Abbey** (Ontario)
- Hundreds of local municipal and private courses designed by local members.

If your local track isn't on there, you can actually design it yourself using the built-in course designer tool.

## Online Play and Community

TGC 2019 has the most robust online community in the simulator world.
- **Society Play:** Join virtual country clubs and compete in weekly events.
- **Live Multiplayer:** Play head-to-head with friends across the country with voice chat.
- **Leaderboards:** Global tracking of scores on every course.

## Pros and Cons of TGC 2019

### Pros
- Astounding library of 150,000+ courses, including tons of Canadian content.
- Exceptional value with the one-time purchase model.
- Lower PC hardware requirements save money.
- World-class course designer tool.

### Cons
- Graphics are beginning to show their age compared to GSPro.
- Short game and putting physics are sometimes critiqued for lack of realism.
- No longer receiving major physics or engine updates (as HB Studios moved on to PGA Tour 2K series for consoles).

## Conclusion: Is TGC 2019 Still Relevant?

Absolutely. For Canadian golfers building a home simulator on a budget, TGC 2019 remains a top-tier choice. The ability to pay a one-time fee of ~$1,300 CAD and instantly access Cabot Cliffs, Pebble Beach, and Augusta National without ever paying a subscription fee again is an undeniable value proposition. 

While GSPro is the new enthusiast favorite, TGC 2019 requires far less computer horsepower and offers a plug-and-play reliability that has stood the test of time. If you want endless variety and hate recurring bills, TGC 2019 is exactly what you need for your Canadian winter golf escape.
