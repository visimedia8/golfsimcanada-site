# Best Golf Simulator Packages Canada (2026): Top Bundles Compared

When outfitting a basement, garage, or commercial facility in Canada, piecing together a golf simulator component by component can lead to compatibility issues, hidden shipping fees, and extreme brokerage tariffs. The most effective route for a Canadian golfer is purchasing a curated **golf simulator package**.

In this master guide, we dissect the absolute best golf simulator bundles available for the Canadian market. We analyze exact CAD pricing, dimensions in feet and meters, and the top Canadian and cross-border distributors (like Par2Pro and The Indoor Golf Shop).

## Why Buy a Package in Canada?

1. **Guaranteed Compatibility:** Ensure your short-throw projector fills your 16:9 impact screen perfectly without aspect ratio distortion.
2. **Freight Consolidation:** Shipping heavy items like hitting mats (which often weigh 50-100 lbs / 22-45 kg) and massive screens from multiple vendors across the US-Canada border will incur multiple UPS/FedEx brokerage fees. Bundles consolidate this.
3. **Support Ecosystem:** One point of contact for warranties.

## Top 4 Golf Simulator Packages for Canadians

### 1. The SkyTrak+ SIG10 Package (Best Overall Value)
The Indoor Golf Shop's SIG10 enclosure paired with the SkyTrak+ is the dominant choice for Canadian garages.

- **Launch Monitor:** SkyTrak+ (Photometric, zero space needed behind the ball)
- **Enclosure:** SIG10 (10'1" W x 7'7" H x 13'7" D / 3.07m x 2.31m x 4.14m)
- **Mat:** Fairway Series 5'x5' (1.5m x 1.5m)
- **Projector:** Panasonic PT-VZ580U (WUXGA)
- **Price Estimate:** ~$11,500 CAD (Duties & Taxes vary by province)
- **Canadian Nuance:** When importing the SIG10 from the USA, be prepared for heavy freight charges. However, the push-button aluminum framing system means no trips to a Canadian hardware store for EMT piping.

### 2. Par2Pro Uneekor EYE XO Studio (Best Premium Canadian Option)
Par2Pro, based in Alberta, is Canada's premier indoor golf supplier. Buying this package eliminates all cross-border anxiety—the CAD price is the final price (plus your local GST/HST/PST).

- **Launch Monitor:** Uneekor EYE XO (Overhead mounted, reads club/ball directly)
- **Enclosure:** Par2Pro SQ Impact Screen with custom side walls
- **Mat:** Fiberbuilt Studio Mat 10' x 10' (Manufactured in North America)
- **Projector:** BenQ LK936ST 4K Laser
- **Software:** Refine+ and GSPro Compatibility
- **Price Estimate:** ~$26,000 CAD
- **Why it wins:** Ships natively within Canada. You get localized tech support from Edmonton, AB, meaning zero time-zone hassles and no border brokerage extortion.

### 3. FlightScope Mevo+ Home Setup (Best for Large Garages)
For Canadians with deep garages (you need a minimum of 16 feet / 4.87 meters of depth), the radar-based Mevo+ provides extraordinary value.

- **Launch Monitor:** FlightScope Mevo+ (Radar)
- **Enclosure:** Carl’s Place Pro Enclosure 8' x 10'
- **Mat:** TrueStrike Single
- **Price Estimate:** ~$9,500 CAD
- **Space Warning:** Radar units need 8 feet from ball to net, and 8 feet from unit to ball. Ensure your garage heater or metallic objects (like snowblowers common in Canadian garages) don't interfere with the radar signals.

### 4. Garmin R10 Retractable Package (Best Space Saver)
If you park your car in the garage during Canadian winters, a retractable setup is mandatory. 

- **Launch Monitor:** Garmin Approach R10
- **Screen:** HomeCourse Retractable Pro Screen 180 (Motorized, rolls up into the ceiling)
- **Mat:** Holy Grail Hitting Strip inserted into EVA foam puzzle tiles.
- **Price Estimate:** ~$5,500 CAD
- **Pros:** Press a button, screen rolls up, park the Honda CR-V inside away from the snow.

## Where to Buy Packages in Canada

1. **Par2Pro (Alberta):** The gold standard for custom Canadian setups. Full turnkey solutions.
2. **Golf Town (Nationwide):** Carries basic SkyTrak and Foresight bundles, though customization is highly limited.
3. **The Indoor Golf Shop (USA):** Excellent bundles, but you must calculate USD to CAD conversion, plus roughly 15-20% on top for freight and provincial taxes.
4. **Carl's Place (USA):** Their "Build Your Own" tool is legendary. Buy the screen and fabric, but buy your 1" EMT pipes at a local RONA, Home Depot Canada, or Lowe's to save on astronomical shipping fees.

## Final Verdict

If your budget is under **$12,000 CAD**, import a **SkyTrak+ SIG10** package or build a Carl's Place custom unit. 
If your budget is **$20,000+ CAD**, work directly with **Par2Pro** for a Uneekor overhead system to enjoy seamless domestic shipping and unparalleled local support.
