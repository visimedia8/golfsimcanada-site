About Golf Sim Canada

Canada's home for independent home golf simulator reviews, Canadian dollar pricing, and verified local installer directories.

Editorial Desk

Our Mission

Building a home golf simulator in Canada comes with unique challenges—from sub-zero winter garage temperatures to navigating customs, CAD currency fluctuations, and localized shipping fees. We built Golf Sim Canada to provide Canadian golfers with clear, zero-bullshit advice, verified CAD pricing, and direct connections to authorized installers across every province.

Editor-in-Chief & Research Team

✎ Editorial Process

We test golf launch monitors, impact screens, short-throw projectors, and hitting mats in real Canadian environments—including unheated garages, low-ceiling basements, and dedicated indoor simulator rooms. Every product review includes hands-on data evaluation and explicit Canadian availability notes.

🛡️ Independent & Unbiased

We are not owned by any golf manufacturer, launch monitor brand, or retail chain. Brands cannot pay for higher ratings or positive review outcomes. Our ratings are calculated strictly on measurement accuracy, value for CAD money, software compatibility, and Canadian warranty support.

💰 Monetization Transparency

To maintain full editorial independence without paywalls, Golf Sim Canada earns revenue through transparent affiliate commissions from authorized Canadian dealers and referral fees when users request quotes from verified local installer partners.

This monetization model does not increase your purchase price in CAD. For full details on our editorial guidelines, visit our Editorial Policy & Monetization Disclosure.
