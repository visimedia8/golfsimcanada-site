Are you planning to build a home golf simulator in Canada but aren't sure if your space is big enough? 

The Golf Simulator Space Calculator is a simple, fast, and free tool designed to help you determine if your garage, basement, or spare room meets the minimum or ideal requirements for indoor golf.
