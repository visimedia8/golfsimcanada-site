# GolfSimCanada.site — Wiki / Glossary Strategy

> **File**: `06c-wiki-glossary.md`  
> **Lead**: PM + SEO  
> **Philosophy**: More detailed and Canada-specific than Wikipedia. Every term has real buyer context, CAD pricing references, and links to relevant money pages.

---

## 1. Why a Wiki (Not Just Definitions)

Wikipedia = generic, no geo, no buyer context.  
**Golf Sim Canada Wiki** = the answer to "what does this mean AND how does it affect my buying decision in Canada?"

Every glossary page answers 3 things:
1. **What is it?** (definition — AEO/featured snippet target)
2. **Why does it matter for your home golf sim?** (practical context)
3. **What should a Canadian buyer know?** (geo-specific — CAD cost, availability, conditions)

## 6. Internal Linking Rules for Wiki

**From articles → glossary**:
- Link to a glossary term on **first mention only** in an article
- Anchor text = exact term (e.g., "smash factor", "launch angle")
- Max 3 glossary links per article (don't turn every term into a link)

**From glossary → money pages**:
- Each glossary term links to exactly **1 primary money page** via contextual CTA at bottom
- Each term also links to **2–3 related glossary terms** (cross-linking within wiki)

**From money pages → glossary**:
- Product pages link to relevant glossary terms in their spec section
- Example: GCQuad page → /glossary/doppler-radar/ + /glossary/smash-factor/

---

## 7. Canada-Specific Depth That Wikipedia Doesn't Have

For each term, include one or more of:
- **CAD pricing context**: "A launch monitor that accurately measures [term] starts at $XXX CAD in Canada"
- **Brand availability in Canada**: "Only Foresight and SkyTrak authorized dealers carry units with [spec] in Canada"
- **Winter/indoor context**: "If your garage drops below -10°C, [component] affects accuracy because..."
- **Import/duty note**: "Buying [brand] from a US retailer means [duty %] at the border — here's the math"
- **Province-specific**: "BC and Ontario have the most authorized dealers for [term]-related products"
- **Canadian golfer context**: "For Canadian golfers playing indoors Oct–April, [term] matters more because..."
