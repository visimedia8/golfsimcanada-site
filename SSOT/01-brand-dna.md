## 1. Brand Essence

### The One-Liner
> **"Canada's Home for Home Golf."**

### The Deeper Truth
Golf in Canada stops for 4–6 months a year. The serious golfer doesn't stop — they build inside. Golf Sim Canada exists for that person: the Canadian who treats their garage, basement, or spare room as a year-round performance space. We are the guide, the trusted advisor, the source of record. We don't sell you anything directly — we help you make the smartest decision possible, then connect you with the right people to make it happen.

### What We Believe (Brand Manifesto)
- **We believe** Canada's winters don't have to mean golf withdrawals.
- **We believe** a home golf simulator is one of the best investments a serious golfer can make — if they buy the right one.
- **We believe** most golf sim buyers get overwhelmed, overpay, or end up with the wrong setup because the information online is either biased or incomplete.
- **We believe** a neutral, expert, Canadian-focused resource can change that.
- **We believe** in real numbers in Canadian dollars, real specs tested in real Canadian garages, and real dealer connections — not commissions dressed up as recommendations.

## 2. Brand Personality

### Archetype: The Expert Friend
Think of the most knowledgeable golf sim enthusiast you know — the one who's built three setups, tested six launch monitors, and actually read the spec sheets. Not a salesperson. Not a manufacturer. A *friend* who happens to know everything about this. That's us.

### Personality Traits

| Trait | What It Means in Practice |
|---|---|
| **Authoritative** | We back every claim with data, specs, or first-hand context. No fluff. |
| **Straight-talking** | We say "this product has a real weakness here" — even if it's a top seller. |
| **Specifically Canadian** | We talk HST, not sales tax. We say "loonie" costs, not USD. We know what -25°C in a garage means for projector mounts. |
| **Depth-first** | We go further than anyone else. Not just "good mat" — but which mat works on concrete vs plywood subfloor in a heated garage. |
| **Warm, not corporate** | We're not a brand manual. We're a trusted voice. Occasional dry humour is fine. |
| **Independent** | We name weaknesses. We compare competitors honestly. We don't hide who pays for listings. |

## 4. Writing Rules (Lexicon & Style)

### Always Say
- **CAD** or **$CAD** — never assume USD
- **"home golf simulator"** not "golf simulator for home" (entity alignment)
- **"launch monitor"** not "launch tracking device"
- **"Canada's [X]"** to reinforce geo-authority
- **"Verified Canadian Dealer"** not "affiliate partner"
- **"Last Verified: [Month Year]"** on all pricing data
- **Province names** — "Ontario," "British Columbia," not just "Canada"
- **"indoor golf"** for venues, **"home golf simulator"** for home installs
- **"Get installation quotes"** not "buy now"
- **"Editor's Choice"** not "sponsored"

### Never Say
- "Click here to buy" (sounds affiliate)
- "Best [X] ever" (superlative without evidence)
- "As an Amazon Associate..." (visible affiliate disclosure is fine but buried)
- USD prices without CAD conversion
- "Golf simulator near me" as a heading (use proper Canadian city names)
- "Cheap" in product context (say "budget-friendly" or "entry-level")

### Canadian English Specifics
- **Spelling**: colour, neighbour, centre, fibre, licence (noun)
- **Currency**: $1,499 CAD or CAD $1,499 — always
- **Dates**: July 29, 2025 (not 07/29/25)
- **Taxes**: Mention HST (Ontario/NS/NB/NL/PEI), GST+PST (BC, SK, MB), GST only (AB)
- **Tone**: Slightly more formal than US copy but warmer than UK copy

## 5. Digital Fingerprint (Linguistic Signature)

These are phrases, sentence patterns, and structural choices that make Golf Sim Canada *sound like us* — not like every other affiliate site.

### Signature Phrases
- *"In a Canadian garage in January..."* — we ground specs in real Canadian context
- *"The honest answer is..."* — editorial independence signal
- *"Last verified by our team in [Month Year]"* — trust signal
- *"Here's what we'd buy at each price point:"* — direct advisor framing
- *"If you're in [Province], note that..."* — hyper-localized
- *"Worth every dollar — if you're in this situation:"* — nuanced, not blanket
- *"What the spec sheet doesn't tell you:"* — depth signal

### Sentence Patterns
- **Lead with the answer.** Don't bury the conclusion. "The GC3 is better for 95% of home users. Here's why."
- **Earn the detail.** State the answer first, then prove it with depth.
- **Use the word "Canadian" deliberately** — not every sentence, but more than competitors.
- **Lists over paragraphs** for specs and comparisons. Paragraphs for editorial voice.

### What Makes Us Sound Different from Competitors
| Competitor Style | Our Style |
|---|---|
| "The GCQuad is the #1 launch monitor" | "The GCQuad leads on accuracy — here's the tradeoff" |
| "Buy now for the best price" | "Find an authorized Canadian dealer" |
| USD pricing with asterisk | Clean CAD pricing with verified date |
| Generic "North America" advice | Province-specific purchasing advice |
| Anonymous "Staff Writer" | Named editor-in-chief with photo + bio |
| Stock manufacturer images | Contextual photography (garage, basement, shed) |

## 6. Brand Voice in French (Quebec)

The `/fr/` section follows the same personality — expert friend, straight-talking, locally relevant. Key differences:

- **Register**: Slightly more formal (Quebec French business convention)
- **Terminology**: `simulateur de golf` (not anglicism), `moniteur de lancement`, `écran d'impact`
- **Local refs**: RONA, Canadian Tire (Tire en français), Decathlon Quebec
- **Geo**: Name Montreal neighbourhoods, Quebec City districts where relevant
- **Tagline FR**: *"Le guide québécois du simulateur de golf maison."*

## 7. Logo & Visual Identity Brief

> Full design system in `08-design-system.md` — this section covers brand direction only.

### Logo Concept
- **Mark**: Abstract golf ball trajectory arc — ascending, forward-moving. Not a stick golfer.
- **Wordmark**: "Golf Sim Canada" — clean sans-serif, two weights
- **Palette Direction**: Deep forest green (authority, Canada) + warm white + electric gold accent (premium, performance)
- **Feeling**: Premium outdoor gear brand meets clean editorial — think MEC + Golf Digest
- **Avoid**: Clip-art golfers, red maple leafs (too on-the-nose), generic sports blue

### Typography Direction
- **Editorial font**: Serif with character (e.g., Playfair Display, Libre Baskerville) — for editorial sections
- **UI/Product font**: Clean geometric sans (e.g., Inter, DM Sans) — for product cards, nav, data
- **Mono accent**: For data tables, specs, prices

### Visual Language
- **Photography style**: Real Canadian interiors — garages, basements, golf sheds in winter
- **Product photography**: Clean white or dark studio, no busy backgrounds
- **Data visualization**: Simple, high-contrast tables — no decorative chart junk
- **Icons**: Minimal line icons — clean and purposeful

## 8. Trust Architecture (What Makes Readers Believe Us)

These are not design elements — they are editorial commitments that build trust over time.

| Signal | Implementation |
|---|---|
| **Named Editor** | Real editor-in-chief with photo, LinkedIn, bio on every article |
| **Last Verified Dates** | Every price and spec shows "Last Verified: [Month Year]" |
| **Editorial Independence Statement** | Visible on About page + footer note |
| **Disclosure** | Clear sponsored/featured labels on paid listings |
| **Authorized Dealer Only** | We only list verified Canadian authorized dealers |
| **Province-Specific Data** | Tax notes, dealer availability by province |
| **Original Research** | At least 1 original data point per pillar (survey, test, calculation) |
| **Correction Policy** | "See an error? Tell us." — email in footer |
