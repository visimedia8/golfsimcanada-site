# GolfSimCanada.site — Business Model & Monetization

> **File**: `02-business-model.md`

---

## 1. Positioning: Branded Media + Marketplace (NOT affiliate)

Golf Sim Canada is not a store. We are the **trusted guide + directory** for home golf simulator buyers in Canada. Monetization flows from that trust — not despite it.

---

---

## 3. Lead Gen Mechanics

- **Form Location**: `/contact/` + `/find/installers/[province]/` + product pages CTA
- **Form Fields**: Name, email, province, city, budget range, space type (garage/basement/shed), timeline
- **Lead Routing**: Manual email forward to verified installer partners initially → automate with n8n or simple Cloudflare Worker later
- **Disclosure**: Form clearly states "Your info will be shared with verified Canadian golf simulator installers in your area"
- **CASL compliance**: Unchecked consent checkbox, clear opt-in language

1. **Golf sim distributor** — Foresight Sports Canada, SkyTrak distributor — wants Canadian digital presence
2. **Golf media outlet** — Score Golf, Golf Digest Canada — wants pre-built SEO asset
